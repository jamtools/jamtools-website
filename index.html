Our Products

Four tools focused on different parts of the music workflow — from capturing ideas to performing live. Most are open source, and they're designed to work together.

Proprietary

JamHub

Collaboration and organization platform with deep DAW integration. Manage sessions, coordinate with collaborators, and keep your projects in sync — all from inside your DAW.

AutoBounce

Automated stem and mix bouncing from your DAW. Define export rules, walk away, and come back to perfectly rendered tracks. Integrates with JamHub for one-click project exports.

PianoCatch

Always-on passive recording for your piano. Runs quietly in the background so you never lose an idea. Scroll back through everything you played and save the moments that matter.

Passive Recording MIDI + Audio JamHub Compatible

ChordLink

Real-time chord communication for live performance. Share chord progressions, key changes, and musical ideas with your bandmates as you play — everyone stays on the same page.

Live Performance Chord Display JamHub Compatible

Built to Work Together

AutoBounce, PianoCatch, and ChordLink all integrate with JamHub — so your recordings, exports, and live sessions flow seamlessly into one workspace. Use them standalone or as a connected suite.

Our Story

Jam Tools started the way most good ideas do — out of frustration. We were musicians spending more time fighting our tools than making music. Bouncing stems by hand, losing improvisations we forgot to record, scribbling chord changes on napkins at rehearsal.

We figured if we could build software for a living, we could build software that actually serves musicians. So we started with the problems we knew best: the annoying, repetitive, creativity-killing friction in the music workflow.

Each tool we've built solves a real problem we've personally hit. AutoBounce came from too many late nights manually exporting stems. PianoCatch was born the day one of us played the best thing they'd ever written — and couldn't remember it five minutes later. ChordLink grew out of the awkward "what chord are you playing?" moments on stage.

We believe the best music software disappears into the background, letting you focus entirely on the music. That's what we're building.

What We Believe

The principles that guide how we build, what we build, and who we build for.

Open Source First

Three of our four tools are fully open source. We believe music software should be transparent, auditable, and community-owned.

Musician-Driven

Every feature starts with a real musical need. We don't build tech demos — we solve problems that musicians actually face every day.

Seamless Integration

Our tools are designed to work together. Data flows between them naturally so you can focus on what matters: the music.

Community Powered

Built with and for the community. We welcome contributions, feedback, and ideas from musicians and developers alike.
