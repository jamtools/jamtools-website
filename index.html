How It Works

Three steps. No workflow changes for the producer. Multiple ways in for collaborators.

1

Producer works as usual

Open your DAW, work on your track, bounce when you're ready. SongDrive picks up the project automatically once you begin.

2

Bounces sync automatically

New exports are detected and picked up without manual uploads. The latest mix is always available to your collaborators.

3

Collaborators contribute stems

Contributors add their parts using a fast in-app overdub flow or their own DAW — whatever fits the task. No setup matching required.

Built around how producers actually work

SongDrive wraps around your existing workflow instead of replacing it. You remain the source of truth for the track. Collaboration happens around you, not in spite of you.

  • Starts from your normal DAW session — no new workflow to learn
  • Detects new bounces automatically — no manual uploading
  • Collect contributions from collaborators without becoming a file manager
