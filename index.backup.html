Our Story

Jam Tools started in a cramped rehearsal space where a group of musicians got tired of losing great ideas to clunky workflows.

We kept asking ourselves: why can't our tools talk to each other? Why does bouncing stems take so many clicks? Why didn't I record that incredible riff I just played?

So we started building. What began as scripts and hacks for our own sessions grew into a suite of tools used by musicians and producers around the world.

We open-sourced most of our work because we believe the best music technology should be built in the open, by the people who use it.

What We Believe

Open Source First

We believe music technology should be transparent and community-owned. Three of our four tools are fully open source.

Built by Musicians

Every feature we ship comes from real-world frustration. We're musicians first, developers second.

Interoperable by Design

Our tools are built to work together. Jam Bridge is the backbone, with Auto Bounce and Chord Spark integrating seamlessly.

Community Driven

Our roadmap is shaped by the musicians who use our tools daily. Join our Discord to help decide what we build next.
